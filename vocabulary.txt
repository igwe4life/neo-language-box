# Vocabulary hints for the speech recogniser.
#
# Whisper accepts a short prompt that biases what it expects to hear. Names and
# terms listed here stop it falling back on acoustically similar everyday words
# ("Samson" -> "Psalms", "Philistines" -> "peasants", "pillars" -> "steal those").
#
# HOW TO USE
#   - One term or short phrase per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
#   - BEFORE EACH SERMON, add the proper nouns from that week's reading:
#     the book, the people, the places. This is the single most effective
#     accuracy change available on this hardware.
#   - Budget: the prompt is capped at 224 tokens and the excess is silently
#     DISCARDED. `python run.py doctor` reports the count. The list below is
#     kept short on purpose so you have room to add your own.
#   - Do not repeat words already in SERMON_PROMPT (Bible, Holy Spirit,
#     resurrection hope, everlasting life, kingdom, Jehovah, congregation...) -
#     duplicates just burn budget.
